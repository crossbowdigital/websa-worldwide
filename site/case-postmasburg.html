    Street lights that taught a town to install them.

    Solar street lighting in Postmasburg, Northern Cape

    Sector
    Energy
    Where
    Postmasburg, Northern Cape, South Africa
    When
    From 2014
    Project value
    R 12 000 000
    Partners
    Lesedi Solar PV, Philips Lighting, NDECOR Trading (Pty) Ltd

    The brief.

    Postmasburg needed reliable street lighting that did not depend on a strained grid. The lighting also had to count towards the local development commitments of the solar plants nearby, which meant the work could not simply be flown in and flown out again.

    What Websa did.

    • Procured solar street lights in partnership with Lesedi Solar PV and Philips Lighting, with NDECOR Trading delivering the project under the WEBSA programme.
    • Hired residents of the Northern Cape and trained them to install the lights, so the workforce was local from the first pole.
    • Managed installation across the town under the direction of Collins Ndewere and Lazarus Magura.
    • Left behind installers who could maintain and extend the system without outside help.

    The result.

    The town got its lighting and a trained local workforce got a trade. The delivery record led directly to a two-year role running procurement, supply and training for the Lesedi and Letsatsi solar plants' development programmes.
